About Amber Grove

Real estate, the way a neighbor would do it

Founded in 2011, we grew from a two-person kitchen-table operation into the region's most trusted boutique agency — without losing the part where we still walk every property ourselves.

Fourteen years, one street at a time

We started Amber Grove because buying or selling a home shouldn't feel like a transaction between strangers. Our agents live in the neighborhoods they sell — they know which block gets afternoon light and which school run beats traffic.

Today our team handles everything from first-time buyer paperwork to full estate sales, but the promise is the same one we made on day one: straight answers, fair pricing, and a home that actually fits your life.
